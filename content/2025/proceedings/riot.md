This position paper / conference talk transcript provides a strategic review of the role of fax technology in enterprises in 2025. We synthesize historical context, current usage patterns, security posture, regulatory drivers, and present a pragmatic transition roadmap. The goal is to equip executives, legal teams, and IT architects with concrete recommendations that balance compliance, operational continuity and modernization velocity.
